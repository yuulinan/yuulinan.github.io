statistical programming to evaluate policy-relevant questions.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em,itemsep=0.5pt,topsep=1pt]
  \item Evaluated the health effects of airborne lead exposure by
    linking Texas birth records with race events, emissions,
    meteorological conditions, and geographic data; developed a
    quasi-experimental design exploiting distance, wind direction,
    exposure timing, and emissions.

    schedules to examine delivery timing and maternal complications.
  \item Analyzed population aging and regional economic growth using
    county-level longitudinal data and instrumental-variable methods
    to estimate how demographic change affects local economies.
  \item Clean, merge, validate, visualize, and analyze large datasets
    from multiple administrative and public sources using R, Stata,
    and Python.
\end{itemize}
